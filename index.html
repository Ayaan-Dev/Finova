FINOVA
Take Control of Your Money.

A smart expense tracker for students and young professionals in India (18–35). Manual entry, no bank login: track income and expenses, set budgets, grow savings goals and get plain-language insights. Every number below belongs to one demo user, Aarav Sharma, for 1–23 November 2026, and adds up across every screen.

Page 07

Dark mode

Deep navy surfaces (#0B0D1A canvas, #151829 cards), lifted accents (#7C75FF primary, #AFA8FF for links) and softened status colours keep text at AA contrast. Category colours brighten slightly so charts stay legible. The same tokens drive both themes.

Every button in the phone works. Try logging an expense with the keypad, tapping chart bars, filtering transactions, deleting with confirmation, or switching Appearance to dark in Settings.

    Motion spec

    • Screen push: 240 ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), 14 px slide + fade.
    • Tap feedback: 180 ms scale to 0.98 on buttons, surface-alt fill on list rows.
    • Progress bars animate width over 600 ms on first view.
    • Dialogs: 200 ms scrim fade + 240 ms scale-in from 0.94. Toasts slide up 12 px, auto-dismiss after 4 s.
    • All motion is disabled when the OS “Reduce motion” setting is on.
